Your entries live in your own tab of the sheet (named after your LDAP). Date and Duration are the sheet’s formulas: the time is stamped when an entry is posted, and Duration runs to the next entry. Main Tracker counts DATA, TEXT LABELING and REVIEW entries on CORE HOURS. Only your latest entry can be removed, since removing one in the middle would leave a gap in your tab; fix older ones with Edit.

Picks come from members' own tabs: entries stamped on the work date, of the chosen types, never breaks or end-of-shift rows. Each episode can be taken only once a day, so the Team Lead and the reviewer never get the same one. By default the pick also skips episodes already in the Reviewers Sheet or taken on an earlier day. Which episodes are taken is kept by the tracker itself; a finished review is written straight into the Reviewers Sheet (annotator, episode, task, work date as Date Evaluated, Date reviewed, you as reviewer, shift, the four categories and notes), in one write, after its last filled row. Perfect and Errors stay the sheet's formulas. An episode shows as Reviewed once the Reviewers Sheet has a review of it dated on or after the day it was taken.
